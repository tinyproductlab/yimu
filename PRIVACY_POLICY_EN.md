### Core Principles: Zero Tracking & Local Sovereignty
YiMu is committed to user privacy. We strictly follow:
1. **No Cloud Upload**: All media files, subtitles, and playback histories are processed and stored locally on your device (`library.json`).
2. **Keychain Security**: All NAS / SMB passwords are encrypted in the local Apple Keychain (`kSecAttrSynchronizable = false`). Never logged or exported.
3. **No Third-Party Analytics**: No advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or analytics libraries.
4. **Network Direct Connect**: IPTV and streaming connect peer-to-peer directly with the provider; no proxying or snooping.

For the full interactive bilingual privacy policy, visit: https://ym.tinylabpro.com/privacy or open `docs/privacy.html`.
